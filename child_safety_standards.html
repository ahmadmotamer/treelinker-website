Child Safety Standards

Last Updated: July 24, 2026

TreeLinker is committed to providing a safe environment for all users and maintaining zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

Our Commitment

TreeLinker strictly prohibits any activity involving child sexual abuse or exploitation, including but not limited to:

  • Creating, uploading, sharing, or distributing Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).
  • Soliciting, grooming, or exploiting minors.
  • Using the platform to facilitate child exploitation or trafficking.
  • Any behavior that violates applicable child protection laws.

Any account found engaging in these activities will be permanently suspended and may be reported to the appropriate law enforcement authorities.

Reporting Harmful Content

Users can report inappropriate content, profiles, messages, or behavior directly within the application using the built-in reporting tools.

All reports are reviewed as quickly as possible. When necessary, reported content may be removed immediately while an investigation is conducted.

Content Moderation

TreeLinker employs moderation measures to help identify and remove content that violates our policies. We continuously improve our detection and review processes to maintain a safe community.

Compliance with Laws

TreeLinker complies with applicable child protection laws and regulations in the jurisdictions where our services are available. We cooperate with law enforcement authorities when legally required.

Protection of Minors

TreeLinker is designed for users aged 13 and above and is not intended to facilitate inappropriate interactions with minors. Users must comply with our Terms of Service and Community Guidelines when using the platform.
